# K–12 Cyber Safety Checklist

## Overview

The K–12 Cyber Safety Checklist is a practical resource being developed as part of the **K–12 Cybersecurity and Data Privacy Education Framework**.

Its purpose is to help educators and school personnel review everyday cybersecurity practices, recognize potential risks, and identify areas where additional guidance or training may be beneficial.

## Objectives

The checklist is intended to:

- Promote cybersecurity awareness among K–12 educators and school personnel.
- Encourage responsible handling of student and institutional information.
- Support safer use of school-issued devices and digital platforms.
- Help identify potential gaps in everyday digital safety practices.
- Provide a starting point for cybersecurity training and improvement planning.

## Proposed Checklist Areas

The checklist is intended to address topics such as:

1. Password security and account protection
2. Phishing awareness and suspicious communications
3. Student data privacy and confidentiality
4. Device security and software updates
5. Safe internet browsing and digital communication
6. Access permissions and responsible information sharing
7. Reporting suspicious activity and cybersecurity incidents
8. Cybersecurity awareness and ongoing training

These areas provide an initial structure for developing practical cybersecurity guidance appropriate for K–12 educational settings.

## Intended Use

Educators and school personnel could use the checklist to review their existing digital safety practices and identify topics that may require further attention.

The checklist may also support discussions about professional development, cybersecurity awareness, and responsible technology use.

It is intended as an educational self-assessment resource rather than a formal cybersecurity audit or certification tool.

## Connection to the Proposed Endeavor

The checklist complements other components of the K–12 Cybersecurity and Data Privacy Education Framework, including:

- The educator survey and exploratory analysis
- The proposed K–12 Cyber Safety Framework
- The Tech Check adaptive assessment prototype
- The cybersecurity-readiness dashboard concept

Together, these components are intended to support a structured approach to cybersecurity awareness, training, and readiness assessment in K–12 education.

## Development Status

The checklist is part of an ongoing framework development initiative. Its proposed content and structure may be refined through additional research, educator feedback, and future pilot testing.

The checklist has not yet been presented as an independently validated assessment instrument or a formally adopted school cybersecurity standard.
