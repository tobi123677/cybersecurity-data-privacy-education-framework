# K–12 Cybersecurity and Data Privacy Education Framework

## Project Overview

The **K–12 Cybersecurity and Data Privacy Education Framework** is an ongoing research and technology development initiative focused on improving cybersecurity awareness, digital safety practices, and data privacy readiness in K–12 educational environments.

The project combines preliminary educator research, cybersecurity education resources, interactive assessment prototyping, and data visualization to explore practical approaches to identifying cybersecurity knowledge gaps and supporting targeted educational training.

The initiative is being developed as part of my proposed endeavor to advance cybersecurity awareness and data privacy education within the United States K–12 education sector.

## Problem and Motivation

K–12 educational institutions increasingly rely on digital learning platforms, connected devices, and online communication systems.

These technologies create a need for practical cybersecurity awareness, responsible information handling, and ongoing digital safety education for students, educators, and school personnel.

This initiative explores how accessible assessment tools, structured cybersecurity guidance, and data-informed educational resources could help address these needs.

## Project Objectives

The framework aims to:

1. Explore cybersecurity awareness and technology training needs among K–12 educators.
2. Develop practical cybersecurity and data privacy education resources.
3. Create interactive tools for identifying cybersecurity knowledge gaps.
4. Investigate approaches to presenting readiness information through accessible visualizations.
5. Establish a foundation for future educator feedback, testing, and potential pilot evaluation.

### 2. Educator Survey and Analysis

[View Educator Survey and Analysis](02-Educator-Survey-and-Analysis/)

A preliminary educator survey was distributed to 50 educators, with 30 responses received, representing a 60% response rate.

The survey explores technology training, educator confidence, cybersecurity awareness, and self-reported preparedness.

Exploratory analysis is documented using R Markdown. The findings are preliminary and are not nationally representative.

### 3. Cyber Safety Checklist

[View Cyber Safety Checklist](03-Cyber-Safety-Checklist/)

This section documents the development of a practical cybersecurity and data privacy checklist intended to help educators and school personnel review everyday digital safety practices and identify potential training needs.

The checklist is being developed as an educational self-assessment resource rather than a formal cybersecurity audit instrument.

### 4. Tech Check: Adaptive Cybersecurity Assessment

[View Tech Check Project](04-Tech-Check-Adaptive-Assessment/)

**Tech Check** is a working web-based prototype designed to explore interactive cybersecurity-readiness assessments for students and educators.

The prototype includes separate assessment pathways and adaptive question-routing concepts. Initial interface designs were created using Figma, followed by web prototype development using Bubble.

### 5. Cybersecurity Readiness Dashboard

[View Readiness Dashboard](05-Readiness-Dashboard/)

The dashboard demonstrates how cybersecurity-readiness indicators and assessment-related information could potentially be visualized to support educational planning.

**The dashboard uses synthetic demonstration data.** Its displayed values are illustrative and do not represent actual school-wide cybersecurity readiness or validated assessment outcomes.
