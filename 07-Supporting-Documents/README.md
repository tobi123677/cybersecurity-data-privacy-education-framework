# Supporting Documents

This section provides an overview of academic qualifications, professional certifications, employment experience, and other credentials relevant to the development of the K–12 Cybersecurity Readiness Framework.

## Supporting Evidence Categories

- **Academic Qualifications:** Degree certificates and academic transcripts supporting my educational background in data science and information systems.
- **Professional Certifications:** Relevant technical certifications and professional development records.
- **Employment Experience:** Documentation of professional experience in information technology, data analysis, and education.
- **Letters of Support:** Professional or academic letters discussing my qualifications, experience, and proposed work.
- **Additional Evidence:** Other relevant documentation supporting my technical background and proposed endeavor.

## Document Availability

To protect personal and confidential information, official transcripts, employment records, letters of support, and other sensitive documents are maintained separately from this public repository.

Relevant supporting documents may be included in the formal EB-2 National Interest Waiver petition evidence package.

## Relationship to the Project

These qualifications and experiences provide background for the research, technical development, and educational objectives of the K–12 Cybersecurity Readiness Framework.
