# Educator Survey and Analysis

## Overview

This section documents preliminary research conducted to better understand technology training, cybersecurity awareness, and data privacy preparedness among K–12 educators.

The research supports the development of the **K–12 Cybersecurity and Data Privacy Education Framework**, an initiative focused on improving cybersecurity awareness, digital safety practices, and technology readiness in educational environments.

## Survey Background

An initial survey was distributed to 50 educators, with 30 responses received, representing a **60% response rate**.

The survey explored topics related to:

- Technology training and professional development
- Educator confidence in using and troubleshooting digital devices
- Cybersecurity training and awareness
- Self-reported preparedness to use technology in educational settings

The survey is intended to provide preliminary insight into educators' experiences and potential training needs.

## Data Analysis

The survey responses are examined using R and R Markdown to explore patterns in technology training, educator confidence, and reported preparedness.

The analysis includes descriptive summaries, visualizations, and comparisons across survey response categories.

These findings are exploratory and are not intended to establish causal relationships or represent all K–12 educators nationwide.
