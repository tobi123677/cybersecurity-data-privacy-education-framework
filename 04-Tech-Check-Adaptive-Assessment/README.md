# Tech Check: Adaptive Cybersecurity Readiness Assessment

## Project Overview

**Tech Check** is a web-based cybersecurity-readiness assessment prototype developed as part of the broader **K–12 Cybersecurity and Data Privacy Education Framework**.

The application is designed to help students and educators assess their understanding of everyday cybersecurity practices, recognize potential knowledge gaps, and explore opportunities for targeted cybersecurity education.

Tech Check demonstrates how interactive assessments could contribute to a more structured approach to cybersecurity awareness and readiness evaluation in K–12 educational settings.

## Key Features

The prototype includes or explores the following capabilities:

- **Role-Specific Assessment Pathways:** Separate assessment experiences for students and educators/staff.
- **Scenario-Based Questions:** Cybersecurity questions focused on common digital safety situations.
- **Adaptive Question Difficulty:** Question pathways designed to adjust difficulty based on user responses, with correct answers leading to more challenging questions and incorrect answers leading to easier questions.
- **Readiness Feedback:** A developing approach to communicating assessment performance and potential learning needs.
- **Dashboard Visualization:** A demonstration interface illustrating how cybersecurity-readiness information could be presented.

## Current Development Status

Tech Check is an early-stage working prototype and has not yet undergone formal educational validation or institutional deployment.

The dashboard includes **synthetic demonstration data**, which should not be interpreted as actual student performance, educator readiness, or school-wide cybersecurity assessment results.

The adaptive assessment logic, scoring methodology, and learning recommendations require further development and testing before they can support reliable educational readiness evaluations.

## Planned Development and Evaluation

Future development priorities include:

- Expanding student- and educator-specific cybersecurity question banks.
- Testing and refining adaptive question pathways.
- Developing more consistent readiness scoring and feedback methods.
- Improving accessibility and usability across devices.
- Expanding the Figma designs to cover additional assessment screens.
- Gathering educator feedback and exploring opportunities for future pilot testing.

## Connection to the K–12 Cyber Safety Framework

Tech Check serves as a practical prototype within the proposed K–12 Cybersecurity and Data Privacy Education Framework.

While the broader framework focuses on cybersecurity awareness, training, and readiness practices, Tech Check explores how interactive assessments could help identify knowledge gaps and guide targeted educational support.

Together with the educator survey, cyber safety checklist, and readiness dashboard concept, the prototype demonstrates a potential approach to integrating cybersecurity education with assessment and data-informed improvement planning.
